# Growth Audit — REFSIX

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/refsix-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic REFSIX already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in REFSIX's own design. Prepared by Adapty, November 2026.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Show PRO at the end of setup, not only as a row in the More menu | CR +10–20% |
| 02 | Make the 14 free days of PRO the second chance when someone closes the paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 03 | Put their goal in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Ask what they want to improve, then read every answer back during setup | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Show what the yearly plan saves: 5,83 € a month, 42% less than monthly | ARPU +10–15% |

The prototype has a Light / Dark switch, English, Dutch and Danish screens, and a 30-second walkthrough. Open it on a phone with `?open=proto` and it runs full-screen.

## Grounding

Every price, rating and feature line comes from REFSIX's own published material: the App Store listing (PRO at 9,99 € / 69,99 €, 4.6 from 281 ratings on the UK store), refsix.com ("trusted in over a million matches"), the REFSIX PRO page and the in-app screens.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for REFSIX.
